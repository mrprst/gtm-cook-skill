# Common B2B buying signals

A starting list to discuss with the user, not a default to copy. Keep a signal only if it happens before *their* buying moment. "Typical source" is a kind of tool, not a vendor.

| signal_type | direction | kind | typical source | ties to a buying moment when... |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hiring_surge | + | valued (postings in N days) | job boards, careers pages | you sell to teams that grow by hiring |
| role_posted (e.g. a new head of X) | + | binary | job boards | the new role owns the problem you solve |
| leadership_change | + | binary | enrichment / people data | a new leader reviews tools in the first months |
| champion_move | + | binary | CRM contacts + people data | a past user joins a new company |
| funding_round | + | valued (amount) | funding databases, news | new money funds growth projects |
| expansion (new site, new country) | + | binary | news, press releases | new operations need new processes |
| tech_change (adopts/drops a tool) | + | binary | technographics | the tool next to yours changes |
| website_visit (pricing, demo pages) | + | binary | web analytics, reverse IP | they are already looking |
| content_engagement | + | binary | marketing automation | weaker intent: cap it |
| review_pain (bad reviews on a theme) | + | valued (rating) | review sites | the pain you solve is visible |
| lost_deal (recent) | − | binary | CRM | calling again too soon backfires |
| competitor_signed | − | binary | CRM notes, technographics | locked in for the contract length |
| layoffs | − | binary | news | budgets frozen |

Rules of thumb to state as assumptions: intent signals decay fast (half-life in days), company events slowly (months); negative signals should decay too, unless they are exclusions in disguise.
